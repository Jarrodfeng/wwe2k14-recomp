**You need your own copy of WWE 2K14 for the Xbox 360.** This download contains no game files: the launcher asks for
the files you copied from your own disc, or a disc image of it.

## Install

1. Download **{{ZIP}}** below and unzip it anywhere, e.g. `C:\Games\WWE2K14`.
2. Start **`wwe2k14_launcher.exe`**. Windows SmartScreen may warn because the program isn't signed: click **More
   info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. The first-run wizard asks for your game files, checks them, finds your save and suggests settings for your PC.
4. Click **PLAY GAME**, **QUICK MATCH** to start straight into a match, or **30 YEARS OF WRESTLEMANIA** to
   pick any match of that mode.

The full guide (copying your disc and your DLC, controls, settings, mods, reporting problems) is `README.md` inside the
zip.

**What you need:** Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), a graphics card with Vulkan support, and your WWE 2K14 Xbox 360 disc
(version 0.0.0.2, no title update), copied to your PC. A controller is recommended.
