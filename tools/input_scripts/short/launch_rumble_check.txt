# Boots, lets the launcher fire, waits for the match scene (M3.PAC) and the match clock, then screenshots every 900

# Session 10's open item: a launched Rumble skips entrants #1 / #2's entrances (titantron opens are in the log).
# Untimed (lanes OK). Needs the frozen user data. on_fail quit. Screenshots rr_* are local only.
